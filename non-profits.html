Non-Profits Services

  • Goodwill

  • •The Goodwill Retail Store and Donation Center in Clinton, Mississippi, serves as a local hub for affordable shopping and community support. It is part of the Goodwill Industries of Mississippi network, which uses retail revenue to fund job training and career services for individuals facing employment barriers.

    •Address: 941 Highway 80 East, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 708-1797

  • Clinton Community Christian Corporation

  • •The Clinton Community Christian Corporation, commonly known as the 4Cs, is a non-profit social service agency that has served the Clinton, Mississippi, community since 1971. It was founded by Mississippi College students and local leaders to provide a coordinated response to the needs of the elderly and families in financial crisis.

    •Address: 2001 West Northside Drive, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 924-9436

  • AMVETS Drop Box

  • •AMVETS (American Veterans) is one of the most inclusive, congressionally chartered veterans' service organizations in the United States. Founded in 1944 by World War II veterans, it has since expanded to represent the interests of approximately 20 million veterans nationwide.

    •Address 410 US-80 & Clinton, MS 39056, 221 Clinton Blvd, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone (800) 735-3720

  • Reaching Our Community Youth and Adult Daycare

  • •Reaching Our Community Youth and Adult Daycare (often abbreviated as ROC) is a non-profit organization based in Clinton, Mississippi, dedicated to supporting the elderly, disabled adults, and their caregivers. It is recognized as a 501(c)(3) Christian-based agency that focuses on maintaining the independence and health of participants while preventing premature long-term care placement.

    •Address: 469 East Northside Drive, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 488-4765

  • Main Street Clinton

  • •Main Street Clinton is a non-profit organization and designated "Mississippi Main Street" community dedicated to the economic revitalization and historic preservation of Clinton’s two primary historic business districts: Olde Towne and the Boulevard District.

    •Address: 281 E Leake St, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 924-5472

  • Magnolia Charitable Foundation

  • •The Magnolia Charitable Foundation is a relatively new nonprofit organization based in Clinton, Mississippi. While it shares a name with several larger state-wide medical foundations, this specific Clinton-based entity is focused on localized human services.

    •Address: 602 Springridge Rd, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 708-1000

  • Pinelake Church

  • •Pinelake Church is a prominent multi-site Christian church in Mississippi, and its Clinton campus was launched in January 2009 and is one of its five major locations. Known for its modern worship style and community-focused mission, the church describes itself as a place to "Learn from Christ, Live in Christ, and Lead others to Christ."

    •Address: 201 Clinton Center Dr, Clinton, Ms 39056

    •Phone: (601) 829-4500

  • Mississippi Center for Police and Sheriffs

  • •The Mississippi Center for Police and Sheriffs (MSCPS) is a unique non-profit organization that bridges the gap between law enforcement and social services. While it provides a "unified voice" and training resources for police and sheriffs, its primary day-to-day operation in the Clinton/Raymond area focuses on housing and emergency outreach.

    •Address: 1500 Raymond Lake Road, Raymond, MS 39154

    •Phone: (601) 260-9876

  • DaySpring Community Church

  • •DaySpring Community Church is a mission-focused, multi-ethnic Christian congregation located in Clinton, Mississippi. Launched in October 2000, the church describes itself as a "biblically functioning community" that prioritizes evangelism and outreach over traditional "religion." Also they are known for its hands-on approach to community service like prison ministry, pro-life ministry, global missions, and community support.

    •Address: 1100 Clonton Business Park Dr, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 924-9292

  • Holy Savior Catholic Church

  • •Holy Savior Catholic Church is a vibrant, non-profit, multi-cultural parish belonging to the Catholic Diocese of Jackson. Established in 1966, it was the first Catholic church in Clinton and currently serves over 350 families in the Clinton and Raymond areas. The community services it offers are focused on outreach, faith-based support, and local aid, including support for Stewpot Community Services in Jackson. The parish provides a Mother's Group for fellowship, a Young at Heart group for seniors, campus ministry for college students, and periodic collection drives for local charities.

    •Address: 714 Lindale St, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 924-6344

  • Second Harvest

  • •In Clinton, Mississippi, Second Harvest is a food and clothing ministry operated by Morrison Heights Baptist Church. While "Second Harvest" is a common name for food banks nationally, in this local context, it is a specific outreach program designed to serve residents of Hinds County.

    •Address: 201 Morison Dr, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 925-6451

  • Morrison Heights Baptist Church

  • •Morrison Heights Baptist Church is a large, mission-driven Southern Baptist congregation in Clinton, Mississippi. It is known for its strong emphasis on biblical teaching, local community service, and extensive global mission work. It is a central hub for community support in the Clinton area with the second harvest a food pantry, SRV an massive annual community service evry july where volunteers complete local construction, cleaning, and landscaping projects, and missions guest house were they provide housing for visting missionaries and church planters.

    •Address: 3000 Hampstead Blvd, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 924-5620
