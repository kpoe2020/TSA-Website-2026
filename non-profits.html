Non-Profits Services

  • Goodwill

  • •The Goodwill Retail Store and Donation Center in Clinton, Mississippi, serves as a local hub for affordable shopping and community support. It is part of the Goodwill Industries of Mississippi network, which uses retail revenue to fund job training and career services for individuals facing employment barriers.

    •Address: 941 Highway 80 East, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 708-1797

  • Clinton Community Christian Corporation

  • •The Clinton Community Christian Corporation, commonly known as the 4Cs, is a non-profit social service agency that has served the Clinton, Mississippi, community since 1971. It was founded by Mississippi College students and local leaders to provide a coordinated response to the needs of the elderly and families in financial crisis.

    •Address: 2001 West Northside Drive, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 924-9436

  • AMVETS Drop Box

  • •AMVETS (American Veterans) is one of the most inclusive, congressionally chartered veterans' service organizations in the United States. Founded in 1944 by World War II veterans, it has since expanded to represent the interests of approximately 20 million veterans nationwide.

    •Address 410 US-80 & Clinton, MS 39056, 221 Clinton Blvd, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone (800) 735-3720

  • Reaching Our Community Youth and Adult Daycare

  • •Reaching Our Community Youth and Adult Daycare (often abbreviated as ROC) is a non-profit organization based in Clinton, Mississippi, dedicated to supporting the elderly, disabled adults, and their caregivers. It is recognized as a 501(c)(3) Christian-based agency that focuses on maintaining the independence and health of participants while preventing premature long-term care placement.

    •Address: 469 East Northside Drive, Clinton, MS 39056

    •Phone: (601) 488-4765
